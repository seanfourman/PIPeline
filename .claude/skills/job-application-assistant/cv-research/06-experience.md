# Professional Experience

The evidence for rules 18 to 25 in `../10-cv-best-practices.md`.

## What to take from it

- **Accomplishments, not duties.** A bullet says what the candidate changed, built, improved or decided, and what came of it. Every large employer and guide says this; none recommends listing responsibilities.
- **The X-Y-Z shape.** A result, measured by something, reached by doing something.
- **Numbers when the profile has them,** scale when it doesn't, never invented.
- **Scope, recency and relevance.** Show the size of what was handled and put the newest relevant work first.
- **Short, specific, varied,** with the technology named inside the bullet.

## Accomplishments, not duties

- **Amazon:** "A successful resume should focus on your actions and results" and "Be sure to include specific stand-out accomplishments of what you've been able to achieve in each role." Responsibilities describe duties, while accomplishments show results that tell the hiring manager something new. Amazon's own example rewrites "Responsible for introducing new tech stack" into a bullet citing a 25% error reduction and a 37% year-over-year drop in customer complaints, adding "the more you quantify, the better". Source: [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/workplace/amazon-job-application-resume-writing-tips).
- **Google:** "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]." Its recruiters' own better versions include "Won second place out of 50 teams in hackathon" for "Won second place in hackathon", and "Grew revenue for small and medium business clients by 10% QoQ" for "Grew revenue for small and medium business clients". Laszlo Bock's 2014 example: "improved portfolio performance by 12% ($1.2M) over one year by refining cost of capital calculations". Sources: [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/bill-murphy-jr/google-recruiters-say-these-5-resume-tips-including-x-y-z-formula-will-improve-your-odds-of-getting-hired-at-google.html), [Quartz](https://qz.com/201088/google-just-revealed-the-incredibly-simple-formula-for-killer-resumes).
- **Microsoft's recruiters:** listing past job descriptions isn't enough. Show what it took to do an excellent job, with "impactful statements that directly speak to [the company's] culture, position, and desired skillset". Source: [Microsoft Life](https://news.microsoft.com/life/make-resume-shine/).
- **IBM's recruiters:** "Standing out is all about what you have done differently and how you added value." "One mistake a lot of people make is to highlight job descriptions on their resume." And: "Write your measurable results and accomplishments, not just your responsibilities." Sources: [IBM's recruiters](https://www.ibm.com/careers/blog/make-your-resume-stand-out-from-the-pile), [IBM's eight mistakes](https://www.ibm.com/careers/blog/eight-common-resume-writing-mistakes-that-keep-you-from-getting-your-dream-job).
- **EY:** "Do focus on your individual contributions, not just a list of job duties." **KPMG:** "be sure to quantify your experiences and accomplishments", as in "lead a team of four; increased sales by 5%". **JPMorgan Chase:** show what you've accomplished, "using measurable data when applicable". Sources: [EY (PDF)](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gl/careers/documents/ey_resume-dos-and-donts-dec-2020.pdf), [KPMG Canada](https://kpmg.com/ca/en/careers/students/applying-to-kpmg.html), [JPMorgan Chase](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/careers/how-we-hire).
- **The Tech Interview Handbook:** "[Accomplishment summary]: [Action] that resulted in [quantifiable outcome]". Show the scope of the job and the skills it took, and list the best achievements rather than many average ones. Source: [Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/).
- **Gergely Orosz** (a former engineering manager at Uber): lead with the impact and put the technologies near the end. **Harvard:** specific, active language, quantified and qualified, with results. Sources: [Orosz](https://dev.to/gergelyorosz/six-principles-your-resume-should-follow-so-recruiters-will-read-it-3a0o), [Harvard](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/).

## Numbers, and what to do without them

- **Do numbers cause interviews?** No rigorous experiment isolates them. The percentages quoted by CV-tool vendors couldn't be traced to a method. What is measured is that CV quality matters: in Bertrand and Mullainathan's field experiment, higher-quality CVs got about 30% more callbacks (for White-sounding names). In Resume Genius's 2024 survey, about 44% of hiring managers named a CV without measurable achievements as a red flag. Sources: [NBER working paper 9873](https://www.nber.org/papers/w9873), [Resume Genius](https://resumegenius.com/blog/resume-help/resume-red-flags).
- **The measures employers name.** Amazon suggests cost savings, process improvements and time saved; KPMG suggests team size and sales. Sources: [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/workplace/applying-at-amazon-7-tips-from-recruiters), [KPMG Canada](https://kpmg.com/ca/en/careers/students/applying-to-kpmg.html).
- **Without a number,** show scale (users, rows, sources, reports, teams), frequency, who used the work, or what it replaced. A number is never made up: `user/CV_RULES.md` forbids inventing one.

## Scope, recency, relevance

- **Amazon** reads experience for its scope, complexity, recency and relevance, and wants the CV to show "your previous titles, years of experience, skills, and level of expertise". Sources: [Amazon: successful candidates](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/workplace/successful-amazon-job-candidates), [Amazon: 7 tips](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/workplace/applying-at-amazon-7-tips-from-recruiters).
- **IBM:** leave out experience that doesn't support the goal, so the CV stays focused. **Harvard:** list entries in reverse chronological order. Sources: [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/careers/blog/eight-common-resume-writing-mistakes-that-keep-you-from-getting-your-dream-job), [Harvard](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/).
- **Name the technology inside the bullet.** IBM: name the technologies used in the experience descriptions. Google: the programming languages used, for each project and job. Sources: [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/careers/blog/eight-common-resume-writing-mistakes-that-keep-you-from-getting-your-dream-job), [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/bill-murphy-jr/google-recruiters-say-these-5-resume-tips-including-x-y-z-formula-will-improve-your-odds-of-getting-hired-at-google.html).

## Verbs, length and rhythm

- **Action verbs.** Harvard groups them by skill (leadership, communication, research, technical, quantitative and more) and asks for a non-narrative style with no personal pronouns. Source: [Harvard](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/).
- **One line.** Google's recruiters: if a bullet spills over by a word or two into a second line, write it shorter so it stays on one. EY: at most two lines per bullet. Sources: [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/bill-murphy-jr/google-recruiters-say-these-5-resume-tips-including-x-y-z-formula-will-improve-your-odds-of-getting-hired-at-google.html), [EY (PDF)](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gl/careers/documents/ey_resume-dos-and-donts-dec-2020.pdf).
- **How many.** Three to five bullets for relevant roles is the common advice. Source: [Career.io](https://career.io/career-advice/how-many-bullet-points-per-job-on-a-resume).
- **Tense.** Standard practice is the past tense for finished work and the present for ongoing work in the current role.
- **Leave something for the interview.** An IBM recruiter: "Do not write every detail in your resume, so you still have something to say and elaborate during the interview." Source: [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/careers/blog/make-your-resume-stand-out-from-the-pile).

## Junior and unrelated jobs

- **Amazon's student recruiter:** don't cut experiences short; list the work you have space for, including relevant university clubs and volunteering, on one page. Source: [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/workplace/applying-at-amazon-7-tips-from-recruiters).
- **IBM:** with little work history, add relevant class projects, side work or competitions. Source: [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/careers/blog/eight-common-resume-writing-mistakes-that-keep-you-from-getting-your-dream-job).
- **Israel's Ministry of Defense,** for those without professional experience: military service, national service, volunteering and youth-movement roles all count, described by the skills they took. Source: [the ministry's guide](https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/ConsultationAndDirection/Employment/Pages/cv-writing.aspx).
- **The user's rules** rank student jobs low and keep the CV focused on the posting, so an unrelated job shrinks to its title and dates or one transferable line.

## Gaps and short stays

- **Gaps.** Surveys disagree on how much a gap hurts (30% in MyPerfectResume's 2025 survey say employers see gaps negatively). The advice agrees that short gaps need no explanation and long ones are best named as what they were. Harvard advises against unexplained gaps. Sources: [MyPerfectResume](https://www.myperfectresume.com/career-center/careers/basics/career-gaps), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2024/12/23/resume-gap-explanation-job-interview-how-to-handle/), [Quartz](https://qz.com/job-search-interview-employment-gap-tips), [Harvard](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/).
- **Job hopping.** In Resume Genius's 2024 survey, 50% of hiring managers hesitated over frequent job changes. Source: [Resume Genius](https://resumegenius.com/blog/resume-help/resume-red-flags).
- **Dates are checked.** EY: "Don't misrepresent dates, a background check will reveal this." Source: [EY (PDF)](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gl/careers/documents/ey_resume-dos-and-donts-dec-2020.pdf).

## By field

- **Data roles.** The data analyst CVs that CV sites hold up as examples name SQL, a named BI tool and a business result, such as a decision that changed or reporting time cut. Sources: [Enhancv](https://enhancv.com/resume-examples/sql-data-analyst), [Novoresume](https://novoresume.com/resources/resume-examples/data-analyst).
- **Software roles.** Google's recruiters ask for the languages per project and a GitHub link, and the Tech Interview Handbook for a project's reach. Sources: [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/bill-murphy-jr/google-recruiters-say-these-5-resume-tips-including-x-y-z-formula-will-improve-your-odds-of-getting-hired-at-google.html), [Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/).
