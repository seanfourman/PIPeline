# Education

The evidence for rules 28 to 30 in `../10-cv-best-practices.md`.

## What to take from it

- The degree, the institution, the years, and the GPA when it helps. The more recent the degree, the more detail it carries.
- Coursework is for students and new graduates only: a few upper-level courses that match the posting.
- Google's recruiters and the Tech Interview Handbook put education first for students and new graduates. In this template the order is the user's choice.
- Certificates count as evidence of a skill, less than experience or projects.

## What goes in

- **Google's recruiters:** for recent graduates, the school, degree, major, GPA, and month and year of graduation. "Generally the more recently you attended university, the more detail you should include here," says Google technical recruiter Jeremy Ong; the further from college, the less is expected. Source: [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/bill-murphy-jr/google-recruiters-say-these-5-resume-tips-including-x-y-z-formula-will-improve-your-odds-of-getting-hired-at-google.html).
- **The Tech Interview Handbook:** the degree, the graduation year (or expected date), the university, and key achievements such as leadership roles, societies, projects or awards. Source: [Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/).
- **KPMG:** schools, majors and minors, GPA and graduation date; relevant coursework and awards are helpful but not critical. Source: [KPMG Canada](https://kpmg.com/ca/en/careers/students/applying-to-kpmg.html).
- **Harvard's templates** cover the degree, GPA, thesis, relevant coursework and study abroad. Source: [Harvard](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/).
- **The template's shape:** `[Degree] - [Institution] (GPA [GPA], [Distinction])` with `[Mon YYYY] - [Mon YYYY]`. The user chose months for degrees on 2026-11-05, after the scorecard showed Google's recruiters ask recent graduates for them; until then degrees showed years only.

## GPA

- **The Tech Interview Handbook:** list it only above 3.50 of 4.00. **EY:** a GPA and graduation date aren't needed after the first full-time job. Sources: [Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/), [EY (PDF)](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gl/careers/documents/ey_resume-dos-and-donts-dec-2020.pdf).
- **Fewer employers screen by it.** Under half did in NACE's 2022 survey, and where they do, 3.0 is the usual cutoff. Source: [NACE](https://naceweb.org/talent-acquisition/candidate-selection/use-of-gpa-as-candidate-selection-tool-falls).
- The user's own CV shows the GPA, which the CV rules decide.

## Coursework

- Career guides agree that relevant coursework belongs mainly to students, new graduates and career changers, and goes once there is a year or so of work. Three to five upper-level or capstone courses that match the posting work best, since introductory ones tell an employer nothing. Practitioner advice. Source: [ResumeWorded](https://resumeworded.com/relevant-coursework-on-resume-key-advice).

## Order

- **Google's recruiters:** education before experience for students and relatively recent graduates, and experience first once someone has held more than a position or two. **The Tech Interview Handbook:** education first while studying or with under three years of experience. Sources: [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/bill-murphy-jr/google-recruiters-say-these-5-resume-tips-including-x-y-z-formula-will-improve-your-odds-of-getting-hired-at-google.html), [Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/).
- **The template** puts education after experience, the user's own choice, so moving it up is the user's decision, not the writer's.

## Certificates and school results

- **Certificates.** Surveys of IT hiring managers say certifications help validate skills, mainly for entry-level candidates, while experience and a track record count more. Most of these surveys come from certification vendors. No survey of the Google Data Analytics certificate itself was found. A certificate is best backed by a project that uses the skill. Sources: [CompTIA](https://comptia.org/blog/4-reasons-employers-look-for-certified-staff), [Dice](https://www.dice.com/career-advice/will-that-certification-actually-get-you-a-job).
- **Matriculation (בגרות) and psychometric scores.** No source was found on whether to list them. Rule 29 follows the general rule that a degree supersedes school results, and a posting can override it.
