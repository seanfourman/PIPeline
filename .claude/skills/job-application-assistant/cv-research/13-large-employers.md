# What the Largest Employers Say

Company by company, in their own words, from their careers sites, recruiters and documentation. The other files in this folder cite these sources rule by rule.

## Where they agree

- Tailor the CV to the posting's requirements, starting with the basic or minimum qualifications.
- Show accomplishments and results, with numbers where they exist, never a list of duties.
- Keep the layout plain and easy to read, on one page early in a career.
- Write nothing the candidate can't explain in an interview.

## Where they differ

- Amazon says one CV can serve similar roles, while Google says to write a new one for every job. The system tailors each.
- Microsoft's recruiters bold key words, while others warn against overdoing it. The template decides.
- Meta keeps one CV per applicant for every role applied for there, so at Meta that one CV must cover them all.
- The GPA threshold: Deloitte says to include it above 3.2, the Tech Interview Handbook above 3.5. The user's CV rules decide.
- How many roles to apply for at one company: Nvidia says three to five at most, its university recruiter two or three, and Amazon up to five.

## Who publishes nothing

- Apple and Netflix publish no CV advice on their careers sites (searched November 2026). Netflix's engineering blog says only that a recruiter or hiring manager reviews the technical background and experience against the requirements, and its internship page says to be ready to talk about the projects on the resume (from the pages' search extracts). Sources: [Netflix's tech blog](https://netflixtechblog.com/demystifying-interviewing-for-backend-engineers-netflix-aceb26a83495), [Netflix internships](https://jobs.netflix.com/careers/internships).
- Most of the Fortune 500 publish nothing either. What they share is the hiring system they screen with, covered in `14-hiring-systems.md`.

## Google

From Google's recruiting videos ("How to: Work at Google – Resume Tips" and "Create Your Resume for Google: Tips and Advice"), as Inc. summarized them in 2019:

- A PDF; no objective; black text in one clean font; no typos; bullets instead of long lines of text; the name and email prominent.
- "You should write a new, tailored resume for every position you apply for", on one page. Technical roles with many relevant projects may run to a second.
- If a bullet spills a word or two onto a second line, write it shorter so it stays on one.
- The X-Y-Z formula, "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]", with the recruiters' own better versions, such as "Won second place out of 50 teams in hackathon" for "Won second place in hackathon".
- Shorthand such as "QoQ" only when the reviewer surely knows it.
- Education before experience for students and recent graduates, with the school, degree, major, GPA and month and year of graduation. "Generally the more recently you attended university, the more detail you should include here" (Jeremy Ong, Google technical recruiter).
- For technical roles: the GitHub link at the top with the contact information, the programming languages listed prominently, and the languages used named for each project.
- Google very rarely asks for or reads a cover letter.

Sources: [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/bill-murphy-jr/google-recruiters-say-these-5-resume-tips-including-x-y-z-formula-will-improve-your-odds-of-getting-hired-at-google.html), [an archived copy (PDF)](https://law.utexas.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/44/2020/09/Google-Recruiters-Say-Using-the-X-Y-Z-Formula-on-Your-Resume-Will-Improve-Your-Odds-of-Getting-Hired-at-Google-_-Inc.com_.pdf). Google's own hiring page carries no CV advice today. Its hiring attributes, as Laszlo Bock described them in 2014 and 2015, are general cognitive ability (the ability to learn), emergent leadership, "Googleyness" and role-related knowledge, which he called the least important. Source: [World Economic Forum, 2015](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2015/04/13-qualities-google-looks-for-in-job-candidates/).

## Amazon

From Amazon's recruiters on its own news site:

- "It's best to keep a resume text-forward, black and white, and simple." Resumes should be "well-organized, use straightforward language, highlight measurable data, and are streamlined for maximum readability".
- "A successful resume should focus on your actions and results." List accomplishments, not responsibilities; "the more you quantify, the better".
- Tailor by the keywords in the posting's "Basic and Preferred Qualifications", though "You don't need a completely different resume for each role you apply to".
- Pick up to five positions whose basic qualifications you fully meet; show measurable results such as cost savings, process improvements and time saved.
- Keep it concise, with "your previous titles, years of experience, skills, and level of expertise", and truthful. Students: one page, including relevant clubs and volunteering.
- Experience is read for its scope, complexity, recency and relevance, and candidates are evaluated by the Leadership Principles, "the framework of how we evaluate potential candidates".

Sources: [4 ways to improve your resume](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/workplace/amazon-job-application-resume-writing-tips), [7 tips from recruiters](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/workplace/applying-at-amazon-7-tips-from-recruiters), [what successful candidates do](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/workplace/successful-amazon-job-candidates).

## Microsoft

From Microsoft's recruiters (2017):

- "It starts and ends with [clean] formatting", with the most important information up front, white space and bullets rather than dense blocks.
- An introductory paragraph as an elevator pitch, instead of the "old-school" objective.
- A short skills section: don't "over publicize" skills by listing everything.
- Show what it took to do an excellent job, not only the tasks, and tailor to the role and company.
- Bold and italicize key words.

Microsoft also says it seeks "candidates that demonstrate a growth mindset, always learning and showing curiosity". Sources: [Microsoft Life](https://news.microsoft.com/life/make-resume-shine/), [Microsoft News](https://news.microsoft.com/en-cee/?p=2228).

## Meta

- "Your resume should be formatted in a way that's easy to read and emphasizes your work experiences, skills and achievements."
- "Review the minimum qualifications to ensure you're qualified for the position and your resume clearly reflects this."
- One CV per applicant on Meta's site, used for every role applied for there.

Source: [Meta's hiring process](https://www.metacareers.com/hiring-process/).

## IBM

- "Standing out is all about what you have done differently and how you added value." "One mistake a lot of people make is to highlight job descriptions on their resume."
- "Look for the keywords in the job description and find a way to integrate those keywords into your resume."
- "Do not write every detail in your resume, so you still have something to say and elaborate during the interview."
- Its eight common mistakes: skills not aligned with the job; irrelevant experience; random skills with no evidence; soft skills listed instead of shown; too little white space; duties instead of accomplishments; no projects or competitions when work history is thin; no proofreading. One page for recent graduates, up to two for experienced professionals.

Sources: [IBM's recruiters](https://www.ibm.com/careers/blog/make-your-resume-stand-out-from-the-pile), [IBM's eight mistakes](https://www.ibm.com/careers/blog/eight-common-resume-writing-mistakes-that-keep-you-from-getting-your-dream-job), [IBM's careers blog](https://www.ibm.com/blogs/jobs/advice-from-ibm-recruiters/).

## Nvidia

- "Make sure that your resume aligns with the position you're interested in." Limit applications to the top three to five roles that best match your interests and experience, and ask an Nvidia employee you know to refer you through the internal job board before you apply. Source: [Nvidia: how we hire](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/careers/how-we-hire/).
- Its university recruiter Linh Nguyen looks, beyond the technical skills, for "communication, problem-solving and reasoning", and for candidates who can show "how they've been able to practically apply their skills," whether "via extracurriculars, volunteering or coursework". She advises focusing on "the two or three roles you're passionate about". Source: [Nvidia's blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-life-linh-nguyen/).

## Intel

- A one-size-fits-all CV buries what's relevant, and the skills a summary names should be backed by the experience below it (as summarized from Intel's 2015 jobs blog). Source: [Intel](https://blogs.intel.com/jobs/2015/02/5-quick-tips-effective-resume).

## The banks

- **JPMorgan Chase:** "Make sure your resume demonstrates the best possible version of you", and show "what you've accomplished, using measurable data when applicable". Source: [JPMorgan Chase](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/careers/how-we-hire).
- **Morgan Stanley:** "It starts with knowing your resume. Be able to articulate everything you've listed without reading or referring to it." Source: [Morgan Stanley](https://www.morganstanley.com/people-opportunities/students-graduates/resources/interview-preparation).

## The Big Four

- **EY:** a professional email; a summary is optional and never "hardworking, team player, good communicator"; the major always, the GPA only until the first full-time job; individual contributions, not duties; bullets of at most two lines; no industry jargon; the most recent job first, with dates that line up; spell check; a PDF; references only when asked; "Don't misrepresent dates, a background check will reveal this"; one page for recent graduates and anyone under five years of experience. Source: [EY (PDF)](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gl/careers/documents/ey_resume-dos-and-donts-dec-2020.pdf).
- **KPMG:** "Be concise. Try to keep your resume to one page."; "be sure to quantify your experiences and accomplishments" ("lead a team of four; increased sales by 5%"); transferable skills such as leadership, teamwork, communication, organization and client-facing skills; "Ensure formatting is consistent." Source: [KPMG Canada](https://kpmg.com/ca/en/careers/students/applying-to-kpmg.html).

## Deloitte, PwC and Accenture

- **Deloitte (US):** "Remember to keep your resume concise and limited to one page." For campus hires, "If your GPA is over 3.2, be sure to include it." Include "relevant keywords and skills, without lying or stretching the truth"; "always use action verbs when describing experiences"; "use bullets instead of paragraphs and maintain a clean format without fancy fonts"; "Quantifying your achievements can make an impact." Awards and recognitions, internships and leadership positions, volunteer work and school projects led all count, and "be sure you can speak fully to your resume". Source: [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/careers/join-deloitte/recruiting-tips/make-your-resume-stand-out.html).
- **Accenture:** a header like "a business card at the top of your resume", with a website or portfolio link; a one- to two-sentence introduction of the most valuable skills and experiences; cut jobs that aren't relevant; the posting's keywords, since recruiters search for them to see "how well your skills match with the job requirements"; and an error-free page another person has read. Source: [Accenture](https://www.accenture.com/us-en/blogs/blogs-careers/4-ways-to-help-your-resume-stand-out).
- **PwC (Czech Republic):** one page, two at most; for graduates, the major and the thesis topic; and dates and details that match the LinkedIn profile, because recruiters compare them. The page blocks automated reading, so this is from its search extract. Source: [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/cz/en/kariera/blog/cv.html).

## Workday, the system behind many large employers

- HiredScore grades each candidate from A to D by how well the CV matches the posting's skills, education and experience. Since August 2026, meeting every basic qualification earns an A or a B, and the preferred qualifications separate A from B. Recruiters are told to review C and D candidates too. Sources: [Workday's documentation](https://doc.workday.com/hiredscore/en-us/workday-hiredscore/recruiter-productivity-/concept--hiredscore-grades.html), [the August 2026 release note](https://doc.workday.com/hiredscore/en-us/workday-hiredscore/hiredscore-release-notes/2026/august/wednesday--5th-august.html).

## Israeli companies

- **Recruiting managers at WalkMe, Gloat, Cervello and accessiBe:** keywords from the posting; the strongest matches in the top lines; never a generic CV sent "blind"; a current LinkedIn profile; about 30 seconds to decide whether to read on; a long, cluttered CV suggests an unfocused candidate. Source: [Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/finances-money/Article-02c0a6072605581027.htm).
- **Israel's Ministry of Defense,** for discharged soldiers: research the organization and the role; translate a military role into the skills it demanded; volunteering, courses, distinctions and scholarships count. Source: [the ministry's guide](https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/ConsultationAndDirection/Employment/Pages/cv-writing.aspx).
- **The health funds, banks and insurers** publish no CV guidance; their postings are the guide.
