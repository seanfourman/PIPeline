# Israel

The evidence for rules 11, 32, 39 and 40 in `../10-cv-best-practices.md`.

## What to take from it

- CVs are in English, written natively, with official English names for employers and institutions.
- Israeli recruiters want what the global employers want: tailored CVs, keywords from the posting, the strongest matches in the top lines, and a short, focused page.
- Military service counts as experience when it is translated into civilian skills.
- The law keeps personal details out of the decision, and reserve duty is among the protected grounds.
- Public-sector tenders are judged on paper against their threshold conditions.

## Language

- **English for high-tech.** Israeli high-tech companies sell to the world, so for most tech roles the CV is in English. An English CV is written, not translated word for word. Sources: [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-3674552,00.html), [Drushim on English CVs](https://www.drushim.co.il/article/118/).
- **No Hebrew CVs.** The system writes CVs in English for every employer, public bodies and health funds included. The template's fonts have no Hebrew letters, and Hebrew CVs are not part of the system. They would also be riskier: Hebrew in a PDF is often stored in visual order and comes out reversed, and right-to-left parsing is weaker in general (in tests on Arabic CVs, Taleo swapped the name with the contact details and reversed dates). Sources: [Towards AI](https://pub.towardsai.net/what-i-learned-building-a-job-matching-system-in-hebrew-reversed-text-i-o-psychology-and-when-to-e8a4d302f9fe), [StylingCV](https://stylingcv.com/blog/the-arabic-resume-problem-why-ats-fails-in-mena-and-how-to-fix-it/).
- **Official English names** (Clalit Health Services, Tel Aviv University) follow the rule of exact names: a recruiter or a search finds the name the employer itself uses.

## What Israeli guides and recruiters ask for

- **Drushim:** one to two pages, four or five lines per role, a PDF or Word file, keywords and exact titles, tailored to each job, and no age or city. Source: [Drushim](https://drushim.co.il/article/13/).
- **AllJobs** covers the same ground. Source: [AllJobs](https://www.alljobs.co.il/Campaigns/CVCenter/CVCenterGuide.htm).
- **Nisha's seven things never to write:** personal details, duties without achievements, typos, clichés such as "שחקן צוות", irrelevant or old experience, ignoring the hiring systems, and vague objectives. Source: [Nisha](https://www.nisha.co.il/7-%d7%93%d7%91%d7%a8%d7%99%d7%9d-%d7%a9%d7%90%d7%a1%d7%95%d7%a8-%d7%9c%d7%9b%d7%aa%d7%95%d7%91-%d7%91%d7%a7%d7%95%d7%a8%d7%95%d7%aa-%d7%97%d7%99%d7%99%d7%9d/).
- **Recruiting managers at WalkMe, Gloat, Cervello and accessiBe:** keywords from the posting, such as a programming language or a project-management tool; the strongest matches in the top lines; never a generic CV sent "blind"; a current LinkedIn profile; about 30 seconds to decide whether to read on; and a long, cluttered CV suggests an unfocused candidate. Source: [Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/finances-money/Article-02c0a6072605581027.htm).
- **LinkedIn.** Israeli guides say to assume the employer will look the candidate up on LinkedIn, so the profile must match the CV. Source: [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-3674552,00.html).

## Military and reserve service

- **The Ministry of Defense's guide for discharged soldiers:** research the organization and the role first; translate a military role into the skills it demanded, such as handling pressure, deciding under uncertainty and coordinating; military service, national service, volunteering, youth-movement roles and military courses all count. Source: [the ministry's guide](https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/ConsultationAndDirection/Employment/Pages/cv-writing.aspx).
- **Israeli CV guides** list the dates, the unit, the role, the responsibilities and the rank, in language a civilian employer understands. Sources: [AllJobs](https://www.alljobs.co.il/Campaigns/CVCenter/CVCenterGuide.htm), [Mako, for discharged soldiers](https://www.mako.co.il/pzm-soldiers/discharge/Article-0d11198686e0431006.htm).
- **Reserve duty** is a protected ground under the equal opportunities law. No source was found on how to present it on a CV.

## The law

- Israel's Equal Employment Opportunities Law forbids discrimination, including when hiring, by sex, sexual orientation, personal status, pregnancy, fertility treatment, parenthood, age, race, religion, nationality, country of origin, place of residence, views, political party and reserve duty. Labor courts have awarded compensation over an interview question about age. A CV has no reason to give any of these. Sources: [Workrights](https://www.workrights.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%A8-%D7%95%D7%90%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F), [Kol Zchut's rulings](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%95%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%94%D7%96%D7%93%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94/%D7%A4%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99_%D7%93%D7%99%D7%9F), [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-3567580,00.html).

## The public sector

- Civil service jobs are applied for through the Civil Service Commission's tender system, and the committee checks the threshold conditions against the documents submitted. Source: [gov.il](https://www.gov.il/he/service/apply_for_a_civil_service_job_via_tenders_system).
- Rule 40 follows: every threshold condition must be visibly met on the CV, with the exact degree and field and dates that add up to the years asked for.

## Low-tech employers

- None of the health funds, banks or insurers publishes CV guidance. Their postings are the guide, and the system reads each one before writing the CV.
