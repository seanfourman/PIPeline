# The Header

The evidence for rules 9 to 11 in `../10-cv-best-practices.md`.

## What to take from it

- The role line is the first title a recruiter reads, so it names the posting's title, or the closest honest one, and a focus from the posting.
- The contact line carries a personal email, the phone, LinkedIn and, for technical roles, GitHub, all as clean links.
- Nothing personal goes on the page: it doesn't help, it can hurt, and in Israel most of it is legally off-limits for the decision.

## The role line

- Titles draw recruiters' eyes first (TheLadders' eye-tracking), and a title that matches the posting's went with a higher interview rate in Jobscan's data (10.6 times in 2025, about 3.5 times in 2024; vendor data). Sources: [HR Dive](https://www.hrdive.com/news/recruiters-spend-74-seconds-looking-at-your-resume/541582/), [Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/interview-rates-study).
- The Tech Interview Handbook recommends a headline of fewer than 10 words that starts with the noun for the role. Source: [Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/).
- The template writes the role line in capitals as `ROLE | FOCUS` and shrinks it when long. The user's rule is that it fits both the posting and the candidate's experience, and need not copy the posting's title.

## Contact and links

- **Google's recruiters:** make the name and email prominent, and for technical roles put the GitHub link, or an equivalent, at the top with the contact information. Source: [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/bill-murphy-jr/google-recruiters-say-these-5-resume-tips-including-x-y-z-formula-will-improve-your-odds-of-getting-hired-at-google.html).
- **The Tech Interview Handbook:** the must-haves are the name, a personal phone, a personal email and LinkedIn; GitHub, a personal website and similar profiles are good to have. Never a work phone or work email. It also lists the city, which this system leaves off by the user's rule. Source: [Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/).
- **EY:** a professional email address, the same one on the CV and every application, and a phone number. Source: [EY (PDF)](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gl/careers/documents/ey_resume-dos-and-donts-dec-2020.pdf).
- **Harvard:** a missing email or phone is among the top five mistakes. Source: [Harvard](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/).

## Nothing personal

- **Harvard** leaves off a photo, age and gender. Source: [Harvard](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/).
- **Photos are a lottery on looks and gender.** In an Israeli field experiment (Ruffle and Shtudiner), attractive men got 19.9% callbacks, plain-looking men 13.7% and men without a photo 9.2%. Women without a photo got the most callbacks, and the authors advise women to leave the photo out; the bias against photos was stronger where photos aren't the custom. An Argentinian study found that the photo itself didn't change callbacks, while attractiveness did. The template has no photo, the hiring systems can't read one, and AI screeners add their own biases, so the rule is no photo. Sources: [American Associates of Ben-Gurion University](https://americansforbgu.org/?p=4268), [IZA discussion paper 6356](https://www.iza.org/publications/dp6356).
- **The law in Israel.** The Equal Employment Opportunities Law forbids discrimination, including when hiring, by sex, sexual orientation, personal status, pregnancy, fertility treatment, parenthood, age, race, religion, nationality, country of origin, place of residence, views, political party and reserve duty. Labor courts have awarded compensation over an interview question about age. Sources: [Workrights](https://www.workrights.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%A8-%D7%95%D7%90%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F), [Kol Zchut's rulings](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%95%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%94%D7%96%D7%93%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94/%D7%A4%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99_%D7%93%D7%99%D7%9F), [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-3567580,00.html).
- **AI screeners' name bias** is one more reason to give nothing personal beyond the name; see `01-how-cvs-are-read.md`.
